What happens after you upload?

  1. The system generates a SHA-256 fingerprint of your image.
  2. OpenCV detects and crops the face.
  3. Up to five AI models analyze the face and return raw scores, not probabilities.
  4. The available model outputs are combined into an overall assessment.
  5. The report separates the assessment from supporting forensic indicators.
