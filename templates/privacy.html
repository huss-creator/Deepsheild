Privacy Notice

This is a plain-language description of what this DeepShield deployment actually does. The operator should review it against local law before public use.

What we collect

  • Account: username, e-mail address, and a salted password hash (never your password).
  • Images you upload (JPEG/PNG, up to {{ max_mb }} MB) and files derived from them: face crops, explanation heat-maps and forensic maps.
  • Analysis results: model scores, forensic indicators, a SHA-256 fingerprint of your image and a signed report.
  • Security log: sign-ins, uploads, deletions and access denials with time and IP address (no image content), kept for {{ audit_days }} days.

How images are used

  • Only to produce your analysis. Images are not used to train or tune models and are not shared with third parties.
  • Images are private to your account. Every image request is checked against your account; stored files are not publicly addressable.
  • Faces are processed only to detect, crop and analyse them. No identification or face matching is performed.
  • EXIF/GPS metadata is read as one forensic indicator and is shown only to you.

How long we keep it

{% if retention_days %}Analyses and their images are automatically deleted {{ retention_days }} days after upload. {% else %}Analyses and images are kept until you delete them.{% endif %} You can delete any analysis at any time from its result page, and delete your whole account (with all images and results) from Account & data. Deletion removes the stored files, not just database rows. Backups made by the operator may retain data for a limited period before they expire.

Please only upload images you may analyse

Do not upload images of people without a legitimate reason. Analysis results are probabilistic estimates, not legal proof, and should not be used on their own to accuse anyone.

Contact

Requests about your data: contact the operator of this deployment (the address in the site footer or your administrator).
