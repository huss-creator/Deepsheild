Privacy Notice

This notice describes this DeepShield deployment. Settings and data-handling practices can vary by operator; contact the site operator if anything here is unclear.

What we collect

  • Account: username, e-mail address, and a salted password hash (never your password).
  • Images you upload (JPEG or PNG, up to {{ max_upload_size|filesizeformat }}) and files derived from them, including face crops, explanation maps, and forensic maps.
  • Analysis results: model scores, forensic indicators, a SHA-256 fingerprint of your image and a signed report.
  • Security log: sign-ins, uploads, deletions, and access denials, with their time and IP address. It does not contain image content and is kept for {{ audit_days }} day{{ audit_days|pluralize }}.

How images are used

  • Your images are used to produce your analysis. They are not used to train or tune models and are not shared with third parties.
  • Images are private to your account. Every image request is checked against your account; stored files are not publicly addressable.
  • Faces are processed to detect, crop, and analyze them. DeepShield does not identify or match faces.
  • EXIF/GPS metadata is read as one forensic indicator and is shown only to you.

How long we keep your data

{% if retention_days %}The operator-run cleanup process is configured to delete analyses and their files after {{ retention_days }} day{{ retention_days|pluralize }}. {% else %}Analyses and images are kept until you delete them.{% endif %} You can delete an analysis from its result page or delete your account and its stored analyses from Account & data. Deletion removes the stored files. Operator-managed backups may retain copies temporarily under a separate retention policy.

Only upload images you have the right to analyze

Do not upload images of people without a legitimate reason. Model scores are raw, uncalibrated indicators—not probabilities. Analysis results are not proof that an image is authentic or manipulated, and should not be used on their own to accuse anyone.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, contact the organization that operates this site through its support channel or administrator.
