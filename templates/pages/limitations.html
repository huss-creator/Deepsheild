BOUNDARY CONDITIONS & CONSTRAINTS

System Limitations

Scientific credibility requires clear boundaries. DeepShield is optimized for digital facial still images. Below is an exhaustive list of operational constraints where analytical efficacy may be reduced.

{# Limitations Grid #}

1. Social Media Re-Compression

When an image is shared across messaging apps (WhatsApp, WeChat) or social networks, lossy compression algorithms discard high-frequency DCT coefficients and destroy sensor PRNU noise. Under extreme re-compression (JPEG quality < 50), forensic residual indicators may become unavailable.

2. Single-Frame vs. Temporal Dynamics

DeepShield evaluates individual still image frames. Video-level anomalies — such as unnatural blink rates, pulse variations (rPPG), and temporal jitter across frames — are not captured in a static analysis and require temporal video architectures.

3. Low-Resolution & Extreme Angles

Faces smaller than 64×64 pixels or rotated at extreme angles (> 45° pitch/yaw) yield insufficient pixel density for CNN receptive fields. DeepShield explicitly flags such faces as POOR_QUALITY and marks the ensemble outcome as UNCERTAIN.

4. Adversarial Perturbations

Adversarial attacks specifically engineered to mislead neural gradient descents can introduce subtle pixel noise that flips CNN predictions without changing visual appearance. DeepShield mitigates this by pairing neural backbones with independent classical forensic filters.

{# Summary Alert #}

How DeepShield Responds to Limitations

Instead of guessing or presenting inflated confidence scores when boundary conditions are reached, DeepShield triggers transparent uncertainty reasons: poor_face_quality, insufficient_models, score_in_uncertainty_band, or model_disagreement.
